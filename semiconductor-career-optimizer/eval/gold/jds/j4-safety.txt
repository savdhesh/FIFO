Verification Engineer – Functional Safety (m/f/d)

Your profile

- Sound knowledge of ISO 26262 and safety mechanisms such as lockstep, ECC and watchdogs
- Experience with fault injection tools and diagnostic coverage
- Good German and English skills

Nice to have: CAN FD or Automotive Ethernet, Python.
